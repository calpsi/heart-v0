# HeartV3 presenter guide

## 75-second pitch

Heart development is a moving three-dimensional process, but most students learn it from flat, static diagrams. HeartV3 is an Apple Vision Pro learning app that places an interactive heart in the learner's room.

Apple Vision Pro is a wearable computer. Learners use their eyes and hands to rotate, resize, open layers, isolate structures, and compare stages instead of trying to reconstruct the anatomy in their heads.

HeartV3 offers two paths. Guided Learning uses 51 short steps to cover ten developmental stages and six congenital anomalies. Free Exploration lets learners move through eight timeline stops and inspect 355 selectable parts at their own pace.

The current prototype contains 16 interactive 3D models and a movable close-up view. My newest update improves the tissue materials and lighting across the complete model set.

This is an educational prototype, not a clinically validated simulation. My next step is expert review and testing whether medical students understand and remember the developmental sequence better than they do with static diagrams.

## 45-second demo route

1. Open Guided Learning and choose one developmental stage.
2. Rotate and resize the model to establish that it is a spatial object.
3. Select one structure, then show Isolate and Cutaway.
4. Separate the layers, then restore the model.
5. Open Free Exploration and move to a different timeline stop.
6. Switch to Anomalies and show one defect.
7. End with the learning point: the student can see what changed, where it changed, and when it changed.

## Simple closing line

HeartV3 turns a sequence students usually have to imagine into something they can inspect, compare, and explain.
