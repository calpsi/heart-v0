# Whole embryonic heart — exterior 3D series

Four intact exterior anatomical illustrations accompanying the earlier `partitioning-v1` cutaways. Each has a complete rear surface, asymmetric ventricular mass, superior/posterior atrial tissue, appropriate major vessel assemblies, textured surfaces, and studio-lit front/rear renders. The developmental ages are approximate **post-fertilization** ages.

## Choose a stage

| Stage | Exterior anatomy represented |
|---|---|
| 01 · Around week 4 | Looped ventricular mass, common atrial expansion, common outflow/aortic sac with branch stumps, sinus venosus and paired inflow horns |
| 02 · Weeks 5–6 | Enlarging ventricular mass, separate atrial expansions and appendages, remodeling common outlet with emerging arterial pathways, right venous inflow and common pulmonary venous entry |
| 03 · Around week 7 | Distinct aortic and pulmonary outflow assemblies, aortic arch and branch stumps, pulmonary branches, ductus arteriosus, caval inflows and pulmonary venous entries |
| 04 · Around week 8 | More elongated leftward apex, clearer ventricular contour, atrial appendages, and the same principal fetal vessel arrangement |

These represent developmental landmarks, not a precisely staged embryo at a particular hour. The larger changes in chamber configuration occur before many of the later internal septation events; exterior differences between stages 3 and 4 are necessarily modest. A week-8 embryonic heart is not an adult heart with mature surface fat or a large adult coronary tree, so those adult features are not added.

## Files

- `blender/`: editable scenes with separate named anatomy, packed PBR images, preview camera and studio lights. Opens in the anterior presentation view.
- `glb/`: portable models with embedded textures and independent anatomy nodes.
- `usdz/`: Apple/RealityKit-compatible models with embedded image textures.
- `fbx/`: FBX models with embedded textures, matching the reference files' general interchange format.
- `textures/`: original 1024 × 1024 base-color, roughness and tangent-normal maps.
- `previews/`: transparent 1400 × 1700 front and rear PNGs plus comparison sheets.
- `manifests/`: anatomical part names, units, limitations and per-mesh checks.
- `references/`: rendered inspection thumbnails of the user's reference files.
- `reference-inspection.json`: reference mesh counts and material connections.
- `tools/`: reproducible generation and validation scripts.

Use the `.blend` files for editing or rendering. Use GLB/USDZ for retaining the portable PBR appearance in viewers. FBX appearance depends on the importing application's material conversion. Import the hierarchy as separate entities rather than flattening it if individual part selection is needed.

The models are enlarged teaching objects in metres, approximately 20–23 cm tall including vessel stumps, not embryo life size. Blender: +X anatomical left, -Y anterior, +Z superior. GLB/USDZ: +X anatomical left, +Y superior, +Z anterior. The camera shows an oblique anterior or posterior view, so the left/right display differs between views.

## Tripo references inspected

The FBXs in these sibling directories were read and rendered for comparison without modifying their source files:

- `anatomical+blood+vessel+3d+model`
- `aorta+3d+model`
- `vascular+anatomy+model+3d`
- `penis+anatomy+model+3d` — surface/material reference only, not heart anatomy

They contain roughly 0.98–1.93 million triangles per inspected mesh and use image-connected Principled materials. The new models use the same general approach: a dense smooth surface, irregular tissue relief, warm red/pink and blue/violet identification colors, image-based color/normal/roughness, and broad glossy highlights. Their geometry and texture pixels are original, generated in Blender; they were not generated by Tripo or extracted from the unrelated anatomy. Metallic is fixed at zero for the new tissue materials.

## What “whole exterior” includes

The entire outer heart and the displayed proximal vessel assemblies are modeled, so these can be rotated and rendered from any direction. Vessel stumps have genuine hollow lumen geometry and curved branch junctions. Vessels are truncated for an isolated-organ presentation; the surrounding embryonic body, lungs and complete arch-artery remodeling network are omitted.

**These exterior sculptures do not contain a complete reconstructed interior.** Chamber volumes are exterior tissue masses; their internal valves, endocardium and septa are not duplicated here. Use `partitioning-v1` for the internal teaching views. The two sets illustrate corresponding stages but do not share a registered mesh or identical outer silhouette, so do not overlay them or treat them as matching halves of one specimen. Individual vessel assemblies overlap at attachment sites; they are not a validated continuous blood-flow domain.

Red, blue and violet distinguish structures and match the reference style. They do not encode literal fetal oxygen saturation. These are original illustrative anatomical models, not specimen-derived reconstructions or clinically validated assets. An embryology specialist should review the anatomy and timing before assessed teaching use.

## Sources for developmental form

- [Human Cardiac Development, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38884703/) — staged 3D human embryonic hearts from 3.5 through 8 weeks.
- [A pictorial account of the human embryonic heart between 3.5 and 8 weeks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8917235/) — staged changes in chamber and vessel relationships.
- [Development of the outflow tract and closure of the interventricular septum](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7223678/) — outflow/septation relationships in the Carnegie collection.
- [UNSW Heart Development](https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/index.php/Cardiovascular_System_-_Heart_Development) — developmental overview and terminology.
- [Developmental progression of coronary vasculature](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4715710/) — why a mature adult surface coronary network is inappropriate for these stages.

## Rebuild

From the heart v3 project folder:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup --python HeartAssets/whole-heart-v2/tools/build_whole_heart.py -- --stage 4
```

Use stages 1–4. No Tripo account or API is required. Blender may need to run outside the macOS sandbox to initialize its rendering libraries.
