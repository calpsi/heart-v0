# Semilunar geometry correction — 20 October 2026

The previous later-reference aortic and pulmonary cusps reused the AV leaflet funnel function: the free margin descended toward the ventricle from a planar attachment. This failed to represent the arterial-facing pockets and curved semilunar hinge lines.

Both later-reference semilunar valves now have three pocket-shaped cusps, low hinge nadirs, raised commissural regions and three-sinus walls. The walls split into removable anterior/posterior panels. One aortic cusp still has three selectable layers, with ventricularis on the ventricular side and fibrosa on the arterial side. The early-cushion geometry is unchanged; its planar support is not displayed in the later state. The previous branching AV chordae remain intact.

Checks: all changed meshes are closed with positive volume; all eight cusp/layer meshes intersect the corresponding root attachment region. The initial fibrosa/root gap was detected and corrected before installation. Rendered later-reference view inspected.

Basis: the primary anatomical study “Clinical Anatomy of the Normal Pulmonary Root Compared With That in Isolated Pulmonary Valvular Stenosis”, https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/S0735-1097%2898%2900089-8 . Its indexed anatomical description identifies semilunar hinges and interleaflet triangles. The publisher full text was inaccessible (403); no specimen dimensions were extracted. Supporting overview: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10713916/ .

Limits: original enlarged educational geometry, not scan-derived or independently anatomically validated. Static partly open configuration, no valve motion or flow simulation. Coronary ostia and intact ventricular-root continuity remain absent. Equalized dimensions and thick tissue layers are illustrative; early cushions and AV leaflets still need reconstruction.
