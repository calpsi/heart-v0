# Mitral leaflet correction — 20 October 2026

The prior reference had two equivalent funnel sectors with tiny circular free margins. The revised later-reference geometry has a shorter anterior attachment arc and greater radial reach, a longer posterior arc with a shorter leaflet, and two posterior margin indentations illustrating three scallops. The new noncircular free margins are shown apart in a static partly open configuration. Four branching chordal trees were reconstructed to reach those margins; both muscles still support both leaflets.

Evidence: human anatomical series, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19379970/ (reanimated and perfusion-fixed hearts), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28893395/ (morphologic variation), and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34311062/ (3D echocardiographic variation). These support distinct anterior/posterior anatomy and variability; the three-scallop example is not universal. No specimen dimensions were extracted or claimed.

The planar annular support, isolated ventricular station, exaggerated thickness, and incomplete aortomitral continuity remain limitations. This is not a stage-matched whole-heart reconstruction or a model of valve function. Early cushions, tricuspid leaflets and previously corrected semilunar geometry are retained unchanged.
