# CS10 candidate review

Not approved for installation. Local manifold and nested-layer checks pass, but they do not validate anatomy.

Compared ventral, cutaway and dorsal renders to the published HDBR CS10 ventral/dorsal views (reference/). The generated candidate is excessively elongated, has artificial Y-shaped pole junctions and rectangular dorsal attachment strips. The source has a much more compact, asymmetric cloak with a broad cranial opening and different pole trajectories. Intersections at generated vessel roots are not a verified continuous lumen.

Keep the current app asset labelled as an illustrative regional map. Do not replace it with this candidate or label this geometry a verified Carnegie-stage reconstruction. Next work must solve source registration/global shape and junction connectivity before surface-detail polishing.

Sources: https://hdbratlas.org/hikspoors-pdf/JH_CS10_new_description.html ; https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-022-03153-x . Reference images retained for comparison, not bundled in app.
