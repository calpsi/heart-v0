# Tricuspid leaflet correction — 20 October 2026

The prior reference used three equivalent funnel sectors. The later reference now shows three unequal leaflet patches with a larger anterior leaflet, shorter posterior leaflet and a distinct septal leaflet on the medial side of this isolated station. Six existing branching chordal trees were reconstructed onto their new free margins. A shallow posterior indentation illustrates one possible margin configuration.

Primary evidence: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16988916/ (107 formalin-fixed adult hearts; anterior largest, posterior shortest), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26133960/ (variable leaflet and chordal architecture, frequent direct septal attachments), and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2345895/ (substantial cusp variation). These are later anatomy references, not embryonic dimensions. None of the model dimensions or attachment arc angles is a measured specimen value.

Remaining anatomical deficiency: the septal leaflet still lacks direct ventricular-septal chordal attachments because this isolated support shell has no reconstructed ventricular septal context. Existing papillary targets are retained; this is a partial shape correction, not a complete right ventricular valve apparatus. Planar annular support and early cushions remain simplified. Mitral and semilunar corrections are preserved.

During candidate validation, a nonmanifold junction in the sixth chordal tree was detected. Junction repair then exposed a small papillary attachment gap. The builder now anchors the end against the measured surface of that existing muscle and repeats mesh/contact validation; failed candidates are not installed.
