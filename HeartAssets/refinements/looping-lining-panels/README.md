# Coordinated lining panels

This draft replaces the four whole junction patches with six removable lining panels: two arterial posterior collars and left/right venous anterior/posterior collars. Anterior lining panels now follow the same `_Anterior` visibility convention as the tissue wall, avoiding retained rings in the front cutaway.

All 40 original part identities remain; the six new lining panels bring the draft to 46 parts. All six new solids are manifold with positive volume. None of the 3,400 local passage probes is obstructed. Export validation confirms the 46 expected object identities. See `lining-validation.json` for results and the exact USDZ hash.

The Blender scene and USDZ are development outputs only; the app asset is unchanged. Local lining seams, separate vessel-wall layers, and the global chamber/outflow shape still require anatomical reconstruction and review against a specified stage. The HDBR CS11 comparison is documented in `../../anatomy-review/cs11-reference-comparison.md`.
