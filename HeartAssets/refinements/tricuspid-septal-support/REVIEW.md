# Direct tricuspid septal support — 21 October 2026

Installed in Heart_09_ValvularRefinement as a later-anatomy teaching reference. Added a selectable regional septal support, two direct septal cords and two replacement mural panels. The original wall panels remain in the early-cushion state.

## Evidence and scope

Anatomical literature informing the attachment pattern:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26133960/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19507468/

The geometry is original and illustrative, not segmented from those specimens. Cord counts, dimensions and the regional shell are not a measured developmental reconstruction. The support is not a complete interventricular septum. Annular geometry, ventricular/root continuity and early cushions still need stage-specific anatomical validation.

## Geometry verification

Both new cords contact the septal leaflet and the regional septal support, without intersecting other leaflets. New meshes are closed with positive volume. All 55 original mesh vertex arrays and early-state part IDs remain unchanged. Replacement wall volume differs by approximately 0.003%. The USDZ contains all 60 catalogue part IDs. The rendered later state was visually inspected.
