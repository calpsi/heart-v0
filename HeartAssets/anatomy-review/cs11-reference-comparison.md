# Carnegie stage 11 comparison — 18 October 2026

Public source: [HDBR Atlas interactive hearts](https://hdbratlas.org/interactive/heart.html), CS11 specimen N662. The public viewer references `heart/cs11-662.glb`. Inspected the file's anatomical labels and rendered it from three directions in temporary storage. Source anatomical regions were defined by the contributors named on the HDBR page in consultation with Deborah J. Henderson.

The model includes labelled aorta, atria, left ventricle, right ventricle, outflow tract and veins. The site describes manually defined chamber regions and vascular lumens. Do not interpret those coloured boundaries as physical dissection planes, or the rendered source surfaces as a microscopic tissue reconstruction.

## Observed mismatch with the current draft

- The draft's long, nearly straight outflow stalk differs substantially from the reference's compact curved outflow region.
- The draft's rounded paired lower swellings and superior atrial swelling are not demonstrated to match the reference's spatial arrangement.
- The draft's generic Y branches omit much of the arch/dorsal-vessel context visible in the reference.
- Patching local junctions improves local mesh geometry but does not repair these global differences.

These are visual comparison findings, not registered measurements: the models have not been aligned on common anatomical axes or landmarks. The next global rebuild must establish that registration and a precise target stage before changing proportions. Do not mark the draft CS11-accurate based on this inspection.
