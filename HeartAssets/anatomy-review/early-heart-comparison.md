## Evidence inspected

- Gilbert, *Developmental Biology*, 6th edition, [Lateral Plate Mesoderm](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9982/): indexed textbook excerpts describe endocardium as a lining continuous with vessels and fusion of paired rudiments. Full chapter access was blocked by the site's browser check; this is not a full-chapter review.
- Hikspoors et al. (2022), [staged human reconstructions](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-022-03153-x), sections CS10–12 and Figure 2 captions: early C-looping precedes more complex looping; the early left ventricle is ventral; the AV canal shifts leftward. Endocardial continuity matters throughout. These are morphological references, not measurements transferred into our meshes.
- [UNSW looping movie](https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/index.php?title=Heart_Looping_Movie): indexed teaching description reviewed; direct playback unavailable (403). Do not record this animation as watched.

## Geometry acceptance criteria for the next replacement

1. Choose one morphological stage, document axes, and compare front, back, lateral and internal views against the same staged reference.
2. Construct a connected endocardial lumen before adding outer tissue. Demonstrate the passage through each vascular junction with cross-sections; closed intersecting tubes are insufficient.
3. Derive adjoining removable panels from shared interfaces. Label teaching cuts as cuts, not natural anatomical partitions.
4. Include only structures supported at the chosen stage. Do not borrow adult coronary vessels, epicardial fat, mature valve tissue or adult myocardial fibre patterns for early embryos.
5. Record omitted embryo context and any enlarged layers. Do not translate a source's revised age estimates into the app's conventional day labels without resolving that convention across the timeline.
6. Check mesh integrity, app import and interaction separately from anatomical review. Neither a successful build nor watertight meshes establish biological accuracy.

## Scope

This pass corrects learning content and representation labels. It does not replace a mesh or certify the existing models. A connected, stage-matched looping reconstruction remains outstanding.
