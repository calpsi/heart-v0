# Teaching anatomy critique — 19 October 2026

Review standard: anatomical topology, developmental appropriateness, tissue identity, vessel connections, and suitability for the stated teaching claim. Reviewed installed previews, catalogues, construction scripts, prior local geometry checks and cited literature. This is an evidence-based AI critique, not clinical certification or histological validation.

## Overall verdict

The 16 original models are regional teaching illustrations. Several support useful narrow concepts, but none should be presented as a fully validated, anatomically complete stage-specific heart. The seven HDBR reconstructions preserve stronger source-stage geometry but are region segmentations, not complete tissue-layer reconstructions. Texture cannot compensate for incorrect connections or invented wall thickness.

## Original models

### Heart_01_CardiogenicRegion — Cardiogenic region
**Teaching scope:** Cardiogenic field diagram. Shows bilateral cardiac precursors and a crescent relationship.
**Critique:** The thick crescent and isolated cords omit the early vascular plexus and tissue folding. They are not measured fields or a complete embryo.
**Required improvement:** Rebuild the vascular network and tissue interfaces from early human sections.

### Heart_02_PairedHeartTubes — Paired heart tubes
**Teaching scope:** Paired-channel layer diagram. Distinguishes endocardial lining, cardiac jelly and myocardial tissue.
**Critique:** Two smooth independent tubes exaggerate regularity. The early human primordium includes vascular networks and a shared developing myocardial environment; the embryo and connecting vessels are omitted.
**Required improvement:** Replace the two-cylinder interpretation with stage-matched networks and folding context.

### Heart_03_PrimitiveHeartTube — Primitive heart tube segments
**Teaching scope:** Unfolded regional map. Teaches the ordering of named inflow-to-outflow regions.
**Critique:** The stacked regions are not one embryonic snapshot. The dorsal opening/attachments and paired vascular poles are missing; region labels span different developmental events.
**Required improvement:** Use a continuous stage-specific lumen and myocardial cloak. The CS10 draft failed shape/junction review and must not be installed.

### Heart_04_CardiacLooping — Cardiac looping (D-looping)
**Teaching scope:** Looping schematic. Shows broad bending and relative chamber regions.
**Critique:** Generic swellings and an elongated outflow differ from the source-stage reconstruction. This geometry cannot establish wall thickness or chamber topology.
**Required improvement:** Use CS11 N662 for the primary lesson; rebuild tissue layers only against registered boundaries.

### Heart_05_AtrialSeptation — Atrial septation
**Teaching scope:** Regional atrial septation comparison. Shows the primary septum, mesenchymal cap, dorsal contribution and later atrial-wall fold.
**Critique:** The recent additions correct missing contributors, but chamber shape and venous entrances remain generic. The later fold is a separate fetal reference, not the early-stage anatomy.
**Required improvement:** Register wall/venous geometry and separate exact developmental snapshots.

### Heart_06_EndocardialCushions — Endocardial cushion fusion
**Teaching scope:** AV canal division diagram. Shows opposing major cushions and division into two AV openings.
**Critique:** Isolated rounded cushions lack the atrial/ventricular junction context. They must not be interpreted as the muscular ventricular septum or mature valve leaflets.
**Required improvement:** Reconstruct their connections to the primary septum, dorsal mesenchyme and AV myocardium.

### Heart_07_VentricularSeptation — Ventricular septation
**Teaching scope:** Ventricular septation diagram. Compares a superior communication with a closure contribution.
**Critique:** Symmetric chamber shells and a large closure patch obscure ventricular asymmetry and inlet/outlet relationships. The patch is not a measured membranous septum.
**Required improvement:** Rebuild chamber topology, outlet alignment and the small fibrous closure region.

### Heart_08_OutflowSeptation — Outflow tract septation
**Teaching scope:** Outflow division schematic. Illustrates a shared outlet and separated arterial pathways.
**Critique:** A fixed twisting partition does not reproduce conal/truncal remodeling. Rounded cusp primordia do not establish valve-root anatomy.
**Required improvement:** Use CS18 N1645 in the lesson; reconstruct cushion and root boundaries before adding detail.

### Heart_09_ValvularRefinement — Valvular refinement
**Teaching scope:** Valve component comparison. Separates cushions from a later leaflet/chordal reference.
**Critique:** These are isolated stations, not valves in an intact embryonic heart. Mature-looking layers and tension apparatus must not be assigned to an early stage.
**Required improvement:** Reconstruct annular, leaflet, chordal and papillary attachments in stage-matched ventricular context.

### Heart_10_CirculatoryTransition — Circulatory transition
**Teaching scope:** Birth-transition diagram. Shows flap apposition and a narrowing ductal lumen.
**Critique:** No pressure or complete circulation is modeled. Functional closure is not immediate anatomical fusion; a PFO may persist.
**Required improvement:** Keep physiological explanation separate from later anatomical remodeling.

### Heart_11_VentricularSeptalDefect — Ventricular Septal Defect (VSD)
**Teaching scope:** Intended perimembranous VSD example. Shows a superior septal opening with limited nearby valve landmarks.
**Critique:** The simplified borders cannot establish the full perimembranous subtype or all valve relationships. It is not representative of every VSD.
**Required improvement:** Reconstruct fibrous borders, tricuspid attachment and aortic continuity.

### Heart_12_AtrialSeptalDefect — Atrial Septal Defect (ASD)
**Teaching scope:** Secundum ASD concept example. Shows missing fossa-floor tissue rather than a persisting flap tunnel.
**Critique:** A round hole and generic rims do not represent all ASD types or their relationships to venous entrances.
**Required improvement:** Keep ASD distinct from PFO; reconstruct venous context and variable rims.

### Heart_13_TetralogyOfFallot — Tetralogy of Fallot
**Teaching scope:** Regional Fallot comparison. Shows VSD, aortic override, RV outflow narrowing and thickened RV wall.
**Critique:** The generic chambers and missing septoparietal trabeculations limit the anatomical mechanism. RV hypertrophy is pressure-related, not the initiating septal displacement.
**Required improvement:** Add the displaced outlet septum and verify the VSD and outflow passages remain open.

### Heart_14_TranspositionGreatArteries — Transposition of the Great Arteries (TGA)
**Teaching scope:** Ventriculoarterial connection diagram. Shows aorta connected to the intended RV and pulmonary trunk to the intended LV.
**Critique:** Two ellipsoid half-chambers lack enough morphology to identify RV versus LV independently. Vessel parallelism alone does not diagnose TGA.
**Required improvement:** Rebuild ventricular morphology and AV valve offsets before claiming complete segmental anatomy.

### Heart_15_AorticCoarctation — Coarctation of the Aorta
**Teaching scope:** Regional coarctation example. Shows actual lumen narrowing near the isthmus and ductal attachment.
**Critique:** Arch geometry, narrowing severity and wall thickness are illustrative. Diffuse arch hypoplasia and collateral networks are omitted.
**Required improvement:** Retain the limited regional concept and specify age and variant before extending it.

### Heart_16_PatentDuctusArteriosus — Patent Ductus Arteriosus (PDA)
**Teaching scope:** Ductal patency comparison. Shows an open ductal connection versus a solid remnant.
**Critique:** A patent fetal duct is normal; PDA concerns persistence after birth. The comparison is not a real-time closure process.
**Required improvement:** Add stage/age context and preserve the distinction between functional and anatomical closure.

## Implemented after this critique

- All 23 catalog entries now include specific teaching scope and limitations, displayed in Model info.
- Replaced unsupported day-specific timing for the paired-channel, unfolded tube, AV-canal and ventricular-septation diagrams. The source looping lesson now identifies CS11 N662 explicitly.
- Qualified the perimembranous VSD subtype claim and TGA ventricular-identification explanation.
- Fallot: added two separately selectable outlet-septal components for the abnormal and comparison states. The abnormal component is shifted anteriorly and cranially; dimensions remain illustrative.
- Fallot passage tests exposed existing aortic-root and ventricular-wall material intersecting the intended VSD. Removed those intersections. Nine VSD paths and nine RVOT paths per state now pass against the full active assembly.
- Full conal malalignment, septoparietal trabeculations and realistic ventricular chamber morphology remain unresolved. The new component is a regional teaching correction, not anatomical certification.
